# Support

Bugs and crashes: open an issue at
https://github.com/ELTXZU/Kyanite/issues/new and attach your Geode log from
`geode/logs`. Say which level you were playing and which GPU you have.

The log line from *Log frame timing* helps too: it shows whether the frame time
is going into Kyanite or into the rest of the game.

Known limits before reporting:

- Levels with Scale, Area/Enter, Advanced Follow or keyframe effects render at
  full speed, but the objects those triggers touch keep their start transform
- Intel integrated GPUs are often slower than vanilla; the mod warns on launch
- The old `iliashdz.bismuth.geode` must be removed, otherwise two renderers load

For everything else, the Discord server: https://discord.gg/MDbQt3w8HC
